PHOENIX-11 BOOT FIX PACK
========================

Situaciya: Windows 11 24H2 (build 26100) na C:, proshivka UEFI,
disk 0 - MBR, zagruzochnyh fajlov net -> "vstavte disk".

PORYADOK:
1) PHX_FIX.bat  - zapusti pervym. Otveti: Y (zacinkivaem) -> Y (tip EF).
2) Na reboot - Y. V boot-menu (F7/F12/Esc) vyberi etot disk
   ili punkt "Windows Boot Manager".
3) Esli ne zavelas - zapusti PHX_DIAG.bat i prishly mne PHX_DIAG.TXT.

Logi (na rabochem stole): PHX_FIX_LOG.TXT / PHX_DIAG.TXT
Kopii logov: koren C:\ i ryadom s batnikami.
